Detail A · About

A bit about me

My name is Duc Le, and most people call me Bill. Most of my work is the part nobody sees: finding shops that can machine a part to print, keeping orders in step with drawing changes, and checking that what arrives is what we asked for. I like that the job sits between engineering, finance, and the supplier, and that a late part means a late tool.

How I got here

  • 2023

    Procurement and contract assistant. Public purchasing under state rules, and my first look at spend data across hundreds of vendors.

  • 2023 to 2024

    Oregon State University

    Financial services assistant. Built a SharePoint onboarding hub and moved a paper approval process into Power Automate. On the side, forecast demand for a local restaurant as a class project.

  • 2024

    BS, Supply Chain and Logistics Management

    Graduated from Oregon State. ASCM certificates in procurement (2023) and planning (2024).

  • 2024 to 2025

    Oliden Technology, Stafford, TX

    Procurement for MWD/LWD components. Built a PO tracking system, cleared an old backlog in SAP, and set up the approved supplier process to ISO and API standards.

  • 2026

    Sourced parts for a rotary steerable and MWD/LWD build with a fixed assembly date, and worked quality issues with machine shops.
